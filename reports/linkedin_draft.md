# LinkedIn draft

I recently built a second fixed-income portfolio project focused less on pricing and more on **exchange product activity and liquidity formation**.

I analysed official Eurex statistics for German and European government-bond futures, looking at monthly volume, average daily volume, quarterly roll months and a newer product-adoption case.

A few things stood out. The German Schatz/Bobl/Bund complex remains very active, with Bund leading the three in Jan–Aug 2026 volume. Activity rises sharply in quarterly expiry months, which shows why futures data need to be interpreted with the roll cycle in mind rather than reading a single month in isolation.

I then looked at Euro-EU Bond Futures (FBEU). The interesting question was not simply whether the contract is well designed, but how a newer derivatives product builds sustainable liquidity: client need, market-maker support, execution quality, cash-market linkage and margin efficiency all matter.

My main takeaway: **product development at an exchange is not only about creating a contract. It is about creating a market around it.**

Tech used: Python, pandas, data validation and structured product analysis.

This is an independent portfolio project based on public Eurex statistics and is not affiliated with Eurex or Deutsche Börse Group.
