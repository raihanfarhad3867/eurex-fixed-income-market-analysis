# Concepts for interview — simple language

## Volume
How many contracts changed hands. High volume means a lot of trading happened, but it does not automatically mean traders are bullish or bearish.

## Open interest
How many contracts remain open. It tells us about outstanding positions, not today's trading alone.

## Liquidity
How easily people can buy or sell without moving the price too much. Volume and open interest are clues; bid/ask spread and order-book depth would make the picture stronger.

## Futures roll
Futures expire. Traders who want to keep exposure close the expiring contract and move to a later contract. This often creates high trading activity around March, June, September and December expiries.

## ADV
Average Daily Volume = monthly contracts / number of trading days. It makes months with different numbers of trading days easier to compare.

## Product-development lesson
A contract can be well designed but still struggle if clients do not need it or if market makers cannot support reliable liquidity. A product analyst therefore looks at both the numbers and the client/execution problem.
